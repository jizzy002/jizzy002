### 👨‍💻 A little about me

I'm **Aldin**, based in **Bosnia and Herzegovina**. I like turning everyday problems and random ideas into real, working software — and exploring how AI can make that process more creative and accessible.

My projects bounce between **Android apps, web experiences, Bluetooth integrations, and practical automation**.
- 🔧 I enjoy solving real-world problems with small, focused tools.
- 🤖 I'm exploring what's possible with AI-assisted development.
- 🏍️ Motorcycles are a big part of my world — and [my website](https://aldjan.com) reflects that.
- 🌍 Based in Bosnia and Herzegovina; building for anyone who finds my work useful.
